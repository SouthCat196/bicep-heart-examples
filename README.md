## What is the bicep heart trend?

The bicep heart is a short vertical clip from TikTok and Reels: someone smiles at the camera, raises one arm into a flex and folds both hands into a heart beside their head, so the bicep becomes the bottom of the heart.

People send it to a partner, a friend having a bad day, or family far away, often with a caption like "I heard you were sad.. have a bicep heart".

## How to make one with Bicep Heart AI

The site walkthrough is on [How it works](https://bicepheartgen.com/#how).

1. Open [bicepheartgen.com](https://bicepheartgen.com/).
2. Upload one photo of one adult person (see [photo tips](docs/photo-tips.md)).
3. Tick the two consent boxes.
4. Sign in with Google or an email code, then buy a pack. Sign-in is required before paying; the site and [pricing](https://bicepheartgen.com/#pricing) are public.
5. Generate. You usually get the clip in a few minutes.
