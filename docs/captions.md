# Caption ideas

The clip from [Bicep Heart AI](https://bicepheartgen.com/) is silent, so add the trend's sound when you post it to TikTok or Reels.

People often send the video to a partner, a friend having a bad day, or family far away. A caption already used with the trend is:

> I heard you were sad.. have a bicep heart

The product tagline also works as a short line:

> Have a bicep heart.

You can keep that wording, or write your own around the same situations. This page is a starting point, not a list of official or guaranteed-to-perform captions.
