# Photo tips

[Bicep Heart AI](https://bicepheartgen.com/) takes **one photo of one adult person**. You also need to tick the two consent boxes before generating.

The points below are common-sense advice, not a published list of official technical requirements. Follow any guidance shown on the site when you upload.

- Use a photo of a single adult. Do not upload a group shot if more than one person is clearly in frame.
- Prefer a clear, front-facing photo. The trend itself is someone smiling at the camera.
- Keep the face and upper body reasonably visible, since the clip is a smile, a flex, and hands folded into a heart beside the head.
- Only upload a photo of someone who has agreed to appear in the clip, and tick both consent boxes.
- If you are unsure, start from the walkthrough on [How it works](https://bicepheartgen.com/#how).
