# FAQ

**What do I get?** An about 7-second, vertical 9:16 MP4 of one person doing the bicep heart move.

**Is there audio?** No. The clip is silent so you can add the trend's sound when you post it.

**What photo do I need?** One photo of one adult person, plus the two consent boxes on the site.

**How long does it take?** Usually a few minutes.

**How do I sign in?** Google or an email code. Sign-in is required before paying. The site and pricing are public; no demo account is needed.

**How much does it cost?** Pay per use, in one-time packs: 1 clip for $4.99, 3 clips for $9.99, or 8 clips for $19.99. See [pricing](https://bicepheartgen.com/#pricing).

**Do credits expire?** No.

**What if a clip fails or times out?** Its credits are refunded automatically. See [refunds](https://bicepheartgen.com/refunds).

**What happens to my photos?** They are deleted after about two days. See [privacy](https://bicepheartgen.com/privacy).

**Is there a free plan?** The product is paid, pay per use. Pricing is listed on the [site](https://bicepheartgen.com/#pricing).

**Is there an API or sample output in this repo?** No. This repository is a written guide only.

**Is this official TikTok or Instagram?** No. Bicep Heart AI is an independent tool, not affiliated with TikTok, Instagram, or the creators of the trend.

**How can I get help?** Email [support@bicepheartgen.com](mailto:support@bicepheartgen.com).
